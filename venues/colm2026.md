## Page limits

- **Submission**: strict upper limit of **9 pages** for the main text, with unlimited additional pages for citations/references. Appendices, the ethics statement, and the reproducibility statement don't count toward the 9-page limit but are each capped at 1 page.
- **Camera-ready**: not stated on any of the pages fetched for this profile. COLM's CFP and Author Guide describe the submission limit in detail but don't separately document a camera-ready page allowance the way NeurIPS (+1 page) and ICML (+1 page) do. **Treat this as unconfirmed** — don't assume a camera-ready bump exists; check the live CFP or ask the program chairs directly before finalizing a camera-ready version.
- The template itself carries an unusually direct warning about margin/geometry tampering, worth quoting because it ties formatting directly to desk rejection: *"The geometry package modifies some page properties when used. This can dramatically change the page margins, leading to severe template violation, and potential desk rejection."*

## Anonymization (double-blind)

- Review process is double-blind: *"reviewers and ACs cannot see author names when conducting reviews, and authors cannot see reviewer and AC names."*
- Submissions must not contain acknowledgments or identifying links (e.g., non-anonymous GitHub profiles/repos).
- Title/abstract changes are locked down after the abstract deadline: minor edits are fine, but *"making major changes is grounds for desk rejection"* — a substantial content change post-abstract-deadline ("submitting a completely different paper") risks the paper "not being reviewed appropriately."

## AI / LLM use disclosure

COLM does **not** run its own independent AI-use policy taxonomy — it explicitly defers to arXiv's external policy instead: *"COLM follows arXiv's guidelines with regard to the use of generative AI tools, including large language models."* This is a meaningfully different model from ICLR 2027's required/recommended checklist or NeurIPS's methodology-significance test — there is no COLM-specific disclosure form to fill in; check arXiv's current generative-AI policy (arxiv.org's own submission-policy pages) as the operative rule if your paper is also going up as a preprint.

Separately, on the reviewing side, reviewers must disclose any LLM use in producing a review, "whether cosmetic or not" — a stricter bar than the author-facing policy, and worth knowing if any author on your paper is also reviewing for COLM.

**Submission integrity tooling**: COLM's homepage states *"COLM gratefully acknowledges support from Pangram and GPTZero to check for hallucinated references, AI-generated papers, and AI-generated reviews"* — i.e., the venue actively screens for undisclosed AI-generated content and hallucinated citations rather than relying purely on author self-report. Treat citation accuracy as mechanically checked, not just an honor-system disclosure.

## Reviewing obligations (reciprocal reviewing)

- **Per-submission**: each submission must contribute one reciprocal reviewer from among its authors; multiple submissions from overlapping author sets cannot share the same reciprocal reviewer unless the authors are otherwise ineligible to review.
- **Per-author**: any author with 4+ submissions is automatically added to the reviewer pool, unless already serving on the program committee/organizing team.
- This mirrors ICML's per-submission/per-author structure fairly closely (also true of ICLR's), though the exact exemption mechanics weren't fully documented on the pages fetched — verify against the live SubmissionInstructions/AuthorGuide before assuming an exemption applies.

## Reproducibility statement (optional)

- Authors are encouraged to include material that helps others reproduce the work — pointers to exact software-package versions, hyperparameters used, clear statement of assumptions, and complete proofs for any theoretical claims.
- Framed as optional guidance rather than a mandatory checklist (unlike NeurIPS's mandatory checklist); capped at 1 page, doesn't count toward the main limit.

## Ethics statement (optional, situational)

- *"Authors can add an optional ethics statement to the paper. For papers that touch on ethical issues, this section will be evaluated as part of the review process."* Placed at the end of the paper, ≤1 page, doesn't count toward the page limit.
- Separately, all participants (not just authors with an ethics statement) are expected to acknowledge and, "whenever necessary," discuss the wider impacts of their work against the COLM Code of Ethics — and must "explicitly acknowledge this during the submission process." COLM explicitly reserves the right to reject or refuse presentation of work found to violate the Code of Ethics "at any point," not only at initial submission.

## Supplementary material

- Supplementary code/data must be self-contained, zipped into a single file, max 50MB.

## Desk-rejection triggers

- Formatting/geometry violations that alter the template's page dimensions — the style file itself frames this as grounds for "severe template violation, and potential desk rejection."
- Major (non-minor) changes to the paper's title/abstract/content after the abstract-submission deadline.
- Ethics Code violations, reject-and-refuse authority reserved by COLM "at any point," not limited to initial screening.
- No exhaustive desk-rejection list was found on the pages fetched for this profile (unlike NeurIPS/ICML, which state fuller lists) — this section may be incomplete relative to what the live CFP actually documents; re-check `SubmissionInstructions`/`CallForPapers` directly before relying on this as complete.

## Template

`templates/colm2026/` — fetched directly from `github.com/COLM-org/Template` (the `2026` release tag: `https://github.com/COLM-org/Template/releases/tag/2026`) on 2026-09-03. Contains `colm2026_conference.tex`, `colm2026_conference.sty`, `colm2026_conference.bst`, `math_commands.tex`, `fancyhdr.sty`, `natbib.sty`, and the repo's own `README.md`. The Author Guide explicitly flags that *"COLM uses a distinct template from other venues"* and warns that imported packages (especially `geometry`) must not alter the template's own page parameters. Copy the whole directory when starting a new paper; never edit the `.sty`/`.bst` files directly.
