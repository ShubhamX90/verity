**On the year**: AAAI-26 (the 40th AAAI conference) has already concluded as of this verification date. AAAI-27 (the 41st) is the currently open, live cycle with a fully-specified policy page as of 2026-09-03, which is what this profile targets, rather than the already-concluded AAAI-26. Once AAAI-27's own submission window has also closed, re-verify against whatever `aaai-28` page exists by then; don't assume these numbers carry forward silently — AAAI's own page-limit mechanics have changed between recent cycles (e.g. AAAI-26 used a flat "7 pages of technical content plus references," AAAI-27 reframes the same limit as "up to 9 pages total, with pages 8–9 reserved for references").

## Page limits

- **Submission**: main PDF up to 9 pages total, with **pages 8–9 reserved exclusively for references** — i.e., up to 7 pages of actual technical content, plus up to 2 more pages that may only contain references.
- The **Reproducibility Checklist** (mandatory, see below) is appended after the references and does **not** count toward the page limit.
- **Ethical statements, if included, must fit inside the 7 content pages** — unlike ACL/ICLR, AAAI does not carve out extra uncounted space for an ethics discussion; it's part of the regular page budget.
- **Camera-ready**: the fetched AAAI-27 submission-instructions page states that a camera-ready deadline exists (check the live page for the current date) but does not itself restate a camera-ready page-count number. AAAI-26's equivalent cycle used 7 proceedings pages plus references/acknowledgments, with up to 2 extra technical-content pages purchasable at $300/page after acceptance — AAAI-27's own page describes the same "prepare with extra length, pay for extra pages after approval" mechanism in general (track-dependent) terms, but this profile could not confirm the exact AAAI-27 Main-Technical-Track camera-ready page count from the pages fetched. Verify the current number directly from the AAAI-27 Author Kit (`templates/aaai2027/`) or the live submission-instructions page before finalizing a camera-ready PDF.
- Submission limit: each individual author may appear on **at most 10** AAAI-27 Main Technical Track submissions (the AI Alignment and AI for Social Impact special tracks are excluded from this count).

## Anonymization (double-blind)

- AAAI-27 submissions are anonymous and reviewed double-blind: "The authors must remove all author and affiliation information from their submission for review, and may replace it with other information, such as paper number and keywords."
- This is a genuine, currently-in-force double-blind requirement for the Main Technical Track — don't assume AAAI is single-blind or non-anonymous by reputation; check the specific track's own call if submitting somewhere other than the Main Technical Track, since AAAI runs multiple co-located tracks (IAAI, EAAI, special tracks) that may set their own rules.

## AI use disclosure

AAAI's AI policy is authorship- and prohibition-based rather than a disclosure-statement/checklist-question model like ARR's:

- **No AI authorship**: "any AI system, including Generative Models such as Chat-GPT, BARD, or DALL-E, do[es] not satisfy the criteria for authorship of papers published by AAAI and cannot be used as a citable source."
- **Disclosure requirement**: "the use of any AI system in the development of an AAAI-affiliated publication is only allowed if its role is properly documented" in the manuscript.
- **What's prohibited**: including LLM-generated text in the paper's own prose is prohibited *unless* that generated text is itself the subject of the paper's experimental analysis (i.e., you're studying the model's output, not using it to write your paper).
- **What's explicitly allowed**: using LLMs to edit or polish author-written text — "this policy does not prohibit authors from using LLMs for editing or polishing author-written text."
- **A confidentiality rule that targets reviewers, not authors of a new submission**: AAAI prohibits uploading *submitted papers under review* into any system that might share them, including "prompting Large Language Models with papers or parts of them" — this governs how reviewers (and anyone else handling a confidential submission) may treat other people's papers, not how you may use AI tools while drafting your own manuscript before submission.
- **Accountability**: "all authors are responsible for the entire content of their papers, including text, figures, references, and appendices" — plagiarism (including unattributed LLM-reproduced text) is investigated and can draw sanctions up to multi-year bans, independent of whether disclosure happened.
- **Camera-ready acknowledgment**: accepted-paper authors must explicitly acknowledge awareness of and compliance with these policies at camera-ready submission time.
- **Practical implication for using this skill**: because AAAI's model is "polishing is fine, generated prose in the paper itself is not (except as an experimental subject), and any AI role must be documented," this skill's prose-editing and compression workflows land squarely in the *allowed* "editing/polishing" category, but anything that drafts new prose from scratch would need to be disclosed as "AI system use in development of the publication" and kept out of the submitted text itself unless clearly marked as the object of study. Keep the same running note of what the skill touched that the ACL-family profiles recommend.

## Reviewing obligations

- "All authors of AAAI-27 submissions will be expected to join the conference's reviewer pool unless prevented by extenuating circumstances." AAAI's own framing calls this a "light load," but it is a real expectation, not opt-in.
- Failure to meet it "may result in sanctions such as paper rejection" — a real, if less mechanically detailed, consequence than ARR's cycle-ineligibility penalty.

## Reproducibility checklist

- Mandatory for every submission: the AAAI-27 Reproducibility Checklist (provided in the Author Kit, `templates/aaai2027/ReproducibilityChecklist.tex`) must be completed and appended to the submission PDF after the references. It does not count toward the page limit.
- Unless a question says otherwise, answer "yes" if the relevant information appears either in the paper or in a technical appendix with an explicit cross-reference from the main text; use a "Reproducibility Checklist" subsection at the end of the technical appendix if an answer needs further explanation.
- AAAI does not have a separate mandatory "Limitations" section the way the ACL family does — reproducibility/limitations-adjacent disclosure is handled through this checklist instead, not through required main-text prose.

## Desk-rejection triggers

- **Placeholder or missing titles/abstracts**: "Submissions that have 'placeholder' (e.g., test or xyz) titles or abstracts (or none at all) will be deleted."
- **Substantial abstract changes between the abstract and full-paper deadlines**: Program Chairs "reserve the right to reject papers without review" if the abstract changes substantially in that window.
- **Multiple-submission-policy violations**: overlapping-author submissions that don't constitute genuinely distinct scientific contributions — explicitly including "parallel near-duplicate submissions (variants rewritten via LLM or cosmetic rewrites to obtain 'multiple lottery tickets')" and plain parallel duplicates — may be "summarily rejected."
- **Simultaneous submission elsewhere**: AAAI-27 will not consider a paper that, as of the paper deadline, is under review, accepted, or already published at another archival conference or journal.
- **Ethics-code violations**: plagiarism (including unattributed or reproduced text, whether AI-generated or not), self-plagiarism, fabricated reviewer identities, or coercion directed at reviewers — penalties range from desk rejection to multi-year participation bans.
- The submission-instructions page, as fetched, does **not** explicitly list an anonymization violation as an automatic desk-rejection trigger the way the ARR/ACL family does — the double-blind requirement is stated as a hard formatting rule, but no separate "revealing your identity gets you desk-rejected" sentence was found on the page used for this profile. Treat it as a serious formatting violation regardless, and don't rely on this profile's silence as license to be casual about anonymization.

## Template

`templates/aaai2027/` — the official AAAI-27 Author Kit, fetched directly from `aaai.org/authorkit27/` on 2026-09-03. Contains both LaTeX (`AnonymousSubmission2027.tex` for the anonymized submission, `CameraReady2027.tex` for the accepted version, `aaai2027.sty`, `aaai2027.bst`, `aaai2027.bib`, `ReproducibilityChecklist.tex`) and Word (`Word/AnonymousSubmission2027.docx`, `Word/CameraReady2027.docx`, `Word/ReproducibilityChecklist.docx`) variants, plus sample figures and rendered PDFs of each. Copy the whole directory when starting a new AAAI-27 paper; use the `AnonymousSubmission2027.*` files for the double-blind submission and switch to `CameraReady2027.*` only after acceptance.
